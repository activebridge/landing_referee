HOW IT WORKS

From kick-off to full time, step by step.

The screen layout never moves. End is always small at the top. The action you use most is always the big button at the bottom. The physical button takes you back.

Step 1

Pick your sport

Open Referee+ and tap Football, Basketball, Handball or Ice hockey. Sports listed under PRO open the 14-day trial or the unlock screen.

Step 2

Pick the format

Choose the age group or competition, for example Adult 2×45 or U13 2×30. The format you used last is highlighted in green, so a weekly fixture is one tap.

Step 3

Tap Kick-off at the whistle

The clock turns green and starts. Football counts up from 00:00. Basketball, handball and ice hockey count down from the period length, for example 10:00.

Step 4 · Football

Stop tallies lost time

When play stops for an injury or delay, tap Stop. In football the match clock never stops: it stays green and keeps running, and a yellow outlined box counts the time lost. Tap Play when play restarts. The total appears as "Added 2 min", ready to signal to the fourth official or announce.

Step 4 · Basketball, handball, ice hockey

Pause freezes the clock

In countdown sports, Pause is a real stop. The digits freeze and turn yellow, and a solid yellow PAUSED bar appears. Tap Resume to restart the clock.

Step 5

Feel the end coming

Two short buzzes mean one minute left. Four buzzes mean the period is over. The clock then holds at the period length in white, and a red box counts the time played beyond it. Your added time sits on the line above.

Step 6

End the period with two taps

At the final whistle of the period, tap End at the top. It turns into a red Sure? for three seconds; tap it again to confirm. If you don't, it goes back to End. A stray tap can never end a half.

Step 7

Half-time runs itself

The break counts down in white, with the next period shown below. Four buzzes when the break is over; a red box counts any overrun. Tap Start to begin the next period. In football the 2nd half starts at 45:00.

Step 8

Full time

After the last period, the watch shows the final time and the added time for each half. Tap Done to return to the sport list.

Good to know

Left the app mid-match?

The match keeps going. Open Referee+ again and the green Resume match row at the top shows the period and live clock. Tap it to return to the match screen.

Four states, recognisable without reading.

Each state changes the clock colour, the box under the clock and the bottom button together, so it reads in sunlight, rain or floodlights, and without relying on colour alone. Yellow and red mean what they mean on a card.

Questions

Does the clock stop when I tap Stop in football?
No. Football is played on a running clock, so the match time keeps counting. Stop only measures the time lost while play is stopped, and adds it to the "Added" total you announce at the end of the half.
What is the difference between Stop and Pause?
Stop (football) tallies lost time while the clock runs on: green clock, yellow outlined box. Pause (basketball, handball, ice hockey) freezes the clock: yellow clock, solid yellow PAUSED bar.
What happens if I tap End by accident?
Nothing yet. End first turns into a red Sure? for three seconds. Only a second tap on Sure? ends the period; otherwise it returns to End.
Do I need to hear the watch?
No. Everything is signalled by vibration: two short buzzes for one minute left, four for the end of a period or break, one tap to confirm a button press.
Can I read it in bright sun?
The match screen is a single large clock in green, yellow, red or white on a pure black background, chosen for maximum contrast on AMOLED displays.
How does the Pro trial work?
Pro sports are free to try for 14 days. After that, unlock Pro once for $4.99 in the Zepp app; it covers up to 3 watches.
Which watches are supported?
Amazfit watches running Zepp OS, from the Band 7, GTR 3 and GTS 3 up to the Balance 2, T-Rex 3 and Active 2. The layout is designed separately for round, square and band screens.
